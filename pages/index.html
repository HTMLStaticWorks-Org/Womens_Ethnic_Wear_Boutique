Living Heritage

The Art of Indian Craftsmanship

Every Zariya garment begins at the loom and matures on the wooden adda frame—celebrating centuries-old textile guilds across Varanasi, Lucknow, Jaipur, and Chanderi.

  1. 01

    Pure Loom-Woven Fabrics

    We source certified pure mulberry silk, breathable cotton-mulmul, handspun Chanderi, and plush micro-velvets prized for natural lustre and skin-kind drape.

  2. 02

    Hand-Traced Traditional Patterns

    Archival paisley ambi, Mughal jali geometry, and botanical bel motifs are hand-pricked onto tracing butter-paper and transferred with natural chalk paste.

  3. 03

    Intricate Zardozi & Resham Handwork

    Master karigars hand-couch metallic dabka bullion, real gota-patti ribbons, abhla mirrors, and silk resham knots stitch by patient stitch.

  4. 04

    Couture Finishing & Attention to Detail

    Bound French seams, soft mulmul inner linings, handcrafted fabric latkans, and generous seam allowances ensure heirloom longevity and impeccable comfort.
